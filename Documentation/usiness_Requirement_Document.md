# Amazon Store Sales, Profitability & Returns Analytics

## Business Problem

The retail business needs a centralized analytics solution to monitor sales, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, shipping efficiency, payment preferences, and returns.

Management needs visibility into which categories, products, regions, and customer segments generate the highest sales and profit. The business also needs to identify loss-making products and return patterns to improve profitability.

## Objectives

- Analyze total sales, profit, and profit margin.
- Track monthly sales and profit trends.
- Identify high-performing and loss-making products.
- Analyze return patterns by category and shipping mode.
- Evaluate customer segment performance.
- Compare sales and profitability across regions and states.
- Analyze payment mode preferences.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for management.
